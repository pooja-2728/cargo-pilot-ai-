⚠ Business Challenges

Key problems faced in maritime freight pricing and route planning operations.

🛣 Manual Route Planning

Route selection depends heavily on manual expertise, spreadsheets, and experience, leading to delays and inefficient routing.

💰 Manual Quotation Process

Freight quotations are generated manually, increasing turnaround time and the risk of pricing errors.

📂 Fragmented Information

Route, pricing, and shipment information are scattered across multiple systems, emails, and documents.

📊 Limited Operational Visibility

Stakeholders lack a centralized view of routes, freight costs, and shipment performance metrics.

🤖 Limited Intelligent Assistance

Traditional systems do not provide AI-driven recommendations for route optimization and freight pricing.

✅ How CargoPilot AI Solves These Challenges

AI Route Recommendation

Automatically recommends the most suitable route based on route data.

Automated Freight Quotation

Generates quotations instantly, reducing manual effort.

Centralized Information

Stores route and pricing information in a single platform.

Real-Time Insights

Provides operational visibility and decision-support analytics.
